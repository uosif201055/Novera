# NOVERA

**novera is a new approach to discovering understanding and organizing knowledge from the ground up**

instead of beginning with complicated scientific terminology novera begins with simple observations of the world and gradually builds more advanced explanations through reasoning experimentation and evidence

its goal is to make knowledge easier to understand without sacrificing accuracy

novera is not limited to physics chemistry biology or any single field

it is an evolving system for exploring everything we can observe investigate and understand

**core principle**

observe first question everything test ideas and build understanding from evidence

**first foundations**

- earth and ground
- sky and wind
- water

these are the starting categories of exploration rather than a claim that everything consists of three elements
