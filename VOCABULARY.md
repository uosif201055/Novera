# novera vocabulary

## approved terminology v1.1

These are approved Novera learning terms. Keep explanations simple and show the familiar scientific meaning when first introducing a term.

| term | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| novera | our system for learning and organizing science |
| lithra | land and everything naturally found on or beneath it |
| veyra | surface |
| kavra | rocks |
| sorra | soil |
| nex | plants |
| aethra | air, wind, and the atmosphere |
| hydria | water and water-related things |
| wera | water itself, the substance H2O |
| mera | materials, what things are made from |
| savi | sand |
| pika | particles, tiny pieces of something |

## usage

- "I drink wera."
- "We are studying hydria."
- "Nex grows in sorra."
- "Kavra can break into smaller pika."
- "Savi is made of small grains."

Hydria is the water category; wera names the substance H2O. Novera terms help teach science and do not change the underlying scientific facts.
