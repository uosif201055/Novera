# novera vocabulary

## lithra terminology v1

These names were approved by the project founder.

| term | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| lithra | land and everything naturally found on or beneath it |
| veyra | surface |
| kavra | rocks |
| sorra | soil |
| nex | plants |

These are Novera vocabulary terms, not replacements for established scientific terminology. Their scientific descriptions will be developed and tested in the lessons.
